# Hardware: moved to Void Hammer

The buying and setup guides for the field kit were written here on 2026-10-17
and moved the same day to **Void Hammer** (`../VoidHammer`), founded by the
author as the family's dictionary of hardware and tutorials:

- `VoidHammer:/guides/field-kit/index.md`: the field kit, and the assumptions
  every kit is built on;
- `VoidHammer:/guides/field-kit/choosing.md`: tiers by device count, what to
  buy by specification, battery arithmetic;
- `VoidHammer:/guides/field-kit/setup.md`: step by step, in the Hammer format;
- `VoidHammer:/research/off-grid-collaboration.md`: the research behind it
  (open-source projects, academic work, phone add-ons, laptops, other ideas).

**What stays in Void Maiz** is what the library's software must do on such a
network: [the field network](/concepts/field-network.md) (the star through the
brain, finding it without broadcast, Android socket binding, the room host,
Q38, Q41, Q42). The line: Void Maiz owns reaching a device; Void Hammer owns
the device.
